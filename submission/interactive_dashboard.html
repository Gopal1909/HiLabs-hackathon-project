Contracting Team Executive Insights & Methodology Summary
Key takeaways from Centene's machine-readable transparency files for California commercial PPO contracting

1. Network Attribution Isolation

Centene's 701.6 MB file is a shared repository containing PPO, HMO, and POS plans. We isolated the 456 PPO plans using CMS HIOS product 67138CA073 and California large-group employer trusts, discarding 654,514 HMO and capitation rows.

2. Zero Address Fabrication

Rates are tied only to NPI and TIN. Rather than expanding rates across dozens of billing locations, we mapped rates to verified physical practicing clinics via CMS NPPES API and CDPH hospital licensing rosters, yielding 99.84% transaction rate-row verification.

3. Market Price Competitiveness

Across 97 peer groups, 53.83% of provider rates fall in Fair Market range (RPI 0.90–1.10), while 25.14% offer Low Cost advantage (RPI < 0.90). 3.25% are extreme premium outliers (RPI > 1.30), highlighting prime renegotiation opportunities.
